## 🛠 Selected Work

### 01 — TD Pay | [Repository](https://github.com/TODD43/td-pay)
**Stablecoin Invoicing & Payment Infrastructure**
A non-custodial stablecoin payment prototype designed around business invoicing, wallet payments, and on-chain transaction verification.
* **Tech:** Solidity, USDC, Base, EVM, ethers.js
* **Includes:** On-chain invoice creation, USDC payment flow, Wallet connection, Payment/Transaction verification, Shareable invoice flow.

### 02 — TD Staking | [Repository](https://github.com/TODD43/td-staking-dapp)
**DeFi Staking & Rewards**
A decentralized staking demonstration featuring smart-contract deposits, withdrawals, and reward accounting.
* **Tech:** Solidity, DeFi, EVM, Web3
* **Includes:** Stake, Unstake, Rewards, Balance tracking, Wallet integration, Smart-contract interaction.

### 03 — TD NFT Ticketing | [Repository](https://github.com/TODD43/td-nft-ticketing)
**NFT Event Pass & Membership System**
A blockchain-based event ticketing and digital membership concept using NFTs.
* **Tech:** Solidity, NFT, EVM, Web3
* **Includes:** NFT minting, Token IDs, Digital ownership, Event passes, Wallet interaction.

### 04 — TD Credential Registry | [Repository](https://github.com/TODD43/td-certificate-registry)
**Blockchain Credential Verification**
A blockchain registry for issuing, verifying, and revoking digital credentials. *(Note: Sensitive source documents are not intended to be stored directly on a public blockchain).*
* **Tech:** Solidity, Hashing, Verification, EVM
* **Includes:** Credential issuance, Verification, Document hashing, Revocation, Public verification.

### 05 — TD SupplyChain | [Repository](https://github.com/TODD43/td-supplychain-dapp)
**Blockchain Provenance & Traceability**
An independent blockchain provenance concept exploring traceable digital records for real-world assets and projects.
* **Tech:** Solidity, Provenance, EVM, Blockchain
* **Includes:** Asset registration, Lifecycle events, Location history, Evidence hashes, Public verification, Provenance records.
> **References:** [0000345 OneApp](https://0000345.oneapp.dev), [RedTree-Ke](https://www.redtree-ke.com). *TD SupplyChain is an independent technology concept. These references do not imply ownership, partnership, endorsement, affiliation, or authorization.*

### 06 — TD RWA Tokenization | [Repository](https://github.com/TODD43/td-rwa-tokenization)
**Real-World Asset Tokenization**
A technical demonstration of controlled real-world asset tokenization.
* **Tech:** Solidity, ERC-20, RWA, EVM
* **Includes:** Asset tokenization, Investor allowlisting, Controlled issuance, Transfer restrictions, Emergency pause, Supply controls.
