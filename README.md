# WHY BLOCKCHAIN?

Blockchain can provide useful properties that traditional databases may not provide on their own:

```text
OWNERSHIP
    ↓
PROGRAMMABILITY
    ↓
TRANSPARENCY
    ↓
VERIFICATION
    ↓
SETTLEMENT
    ↓
AUDITABILITY
```

The goal is not to put everything on-chain.

The goal is to put **the right things on-chain**.
